# All-test intervention effects

`all_test_intervention_effects.png` summarizes all 205 eligible test events
across eight held-out recordings. Each uses recorded ego controls plus a fixed
-8 m/s² braking offset from 1.00 s to 2.00 s. Candidate and frozen
common-physics worlds receive the same prefix sample and exogenous seed.

The three panels show candidate-minus-common rear acceleration during the
braking window, candidate-minus-common minimum ego-to-rear gap, and the change
in whether any collision occurs. Negative acceleration differences mean the
candidate applied extra rear braking; positive gap differences mean greater
minimum clearance. These are behaviour differences, not automatic safety or
quality claims.

`maximum_extra_rear_braking/` is a deliberately non-representative diagnostic
selected by the most extra candidate rear braking. It makes the mechanism easy
to inspect but must be read together with the all-event distribution.
